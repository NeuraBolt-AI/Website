FAQ

Questions, answered.

Are the extensions really free?

Yes. They install free from the Chrome Web Store and stay useful without paying. Pro removes the limits and adds the power features listed above.

What do I get with Pro?

Every Pro feature in every NeuraBolt extension, for £0.99 a month: 600% volume with EQ in NeuraSonic, snooze and sessions in NeuraTabs, unlimited downloads and AI upscaling in NeuraSave, and all-tabs capture, PDF and recording in NeuraShot. New tools are included too.

I've paid. How do I unlock Pro?

If you upgraded inside an extension, Pro switches on there automatically within a minute or two. Either way, we email your Pro code (NB-XXXX-XXXX). To unlock another extension, or another computer, open the extension, click the FREE badge at the top, paste the code and press Unlock.

I can't find my Pro code.

Open the extension you upgraded in and click the PRO badge at the top: your code is shown there with a Copy button. It's also in the email we sent after checkout. Still stuck? Email info@neurabolt.ai and we'll sort it out.

How do I cancel?

Use the manage subscription link in your Stripe receipt, or email us. Cancelling stops future payments and Pro stays on until the end of the month you've paid for.

Do you collect my data?

As little as possible. The tools work on your device. We store an anonymous ID to check Pro, and anything you choose to send us through the feedback form. NeuraTabs can optionally share an anonymous hash of a site you re-categorise to improve grouping for everyone, and you can switch that off. The privacy policy covers each tool in detail.

Which browsers are supported?

Google Chrome on Windows, Mac, Linux and ChromeOS. They also install in other Chromium browsers that use the Chrome Web Store, such as Microsoft Edge and Brave.

Something isn't working.

Use Send feedback at the bottom of any extension's popup, or email info@neurabolt.ai with the extension name and what happened. We read every message. More help is on the support page.
