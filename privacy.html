NeuraBolt Privacy Overview

Last updated: 13 November 2026

NeuraBolt makes small, privacy-first Chrome extensions. Our tools do their work on your device, and we collect as little as we can. This overview covers what applies to every NeuraBolt extension and this website. Each tool's tab above explains exactly what that extension stores and sends.

Who we are

NeuraBolt is an independent business based in the United Kingdom and is the data controller for the information described in this policy. Contact us at info@neurabolt.ai.

What we collect across the suite

  • An anonymous device ID or your Pro code, sent when an extension checks whether you have NeuraBolt Pro. The device ID is a random string created by the extension. It is not linked to your name or browsing.
  • Purchase details, if you buy Pro: Stripe tells us your email address, your Stripe customer and subscription IDs, and whether the subscription is active, so we can switch Pro on and email you your Pro code.
  • Feedback you choose to send through the in-extension form: your message, the extension name and version, and an email address only if you add one.
  • Tool-specific data described in each tab, such as an image URL you choose to upscale in NeuraSave, or a hashed category vote in NeuraTabs.

We never collect your browsing history, the contents of the pages you visit, your audio, or anything you capture with NeuraShot.

Why we use it (legal basis)

  • Providing the service you asked for (contract): checking and unlocking Pro, emailing your Pro code, and running features such as AI Upscale.
  • Improving our tools (legitimate interests): reading feedback you send and, in NeuraTabs, the optional shared category votes.
  • Legal obligations: keeping payment records as required by UK tax law.

Who processes it for us

  • Stripe: payments and subscriptions.
  • Vercel: hosts this website and our small API server.
  • Supabase: our database (Pro status, Pro codes, feedback, hashed NeuraTabs votes).
  • Resend: sends the email containing your Pro code.
  • Replicate: performs NeuraSave's optional AI Upscale on the single image you choose.

Some of these providers process data outside the UK, under the safeguards required by UK data protection law. We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use analytics or tracking on our extensions.

How long we keep it

Pro records are kept while your subscription is active and for up to 6 years afterwards for tax records. Feedback is kept for up to 2 years. Data stored inside an extension stays on your device until you remove the extension.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by emailing info@neurabolt.ai. If you are unhappy with our response you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

This website

neurabolt.ai does not use cookies, analytics or trackers. Our host keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security.

NeuraSonic Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

The short version: NeuraSonic processes all audio entirely on your own device. We never receive your audio, your browsing history or the pages you visit. There are no analytics, no ads and no trackers.

What NeuraSonic does

NeuraSonic enhances the sound of audio and video playing in your browser tabs: it boosts volume, applies a 3-band equalizer, smooths loud peaks with dynamics compression and sets the stereo balance. All of this runs locally using the browser's built-in Web Audio API. Your audio never leaves your computer.

Data stored on your device

To remember your preferences, NeuraSonic saves the following in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device:

  • Your on/off state, volume level, EQ settings and stereo balance.
  • For Pro users, a sound profile for each website you adjust, keyed by the site's hostname. Profiles are saved automatically when you change a setting on that site.
  • An anonymous, randomly generated device ID, your NeuraBolt Pro code if you have redeemed one, and your cached Pro status.

Network requests

  • Pro status. When you open the NeuraSonic popup (at most every few minutes), it asks our server at neurabolt-proxy.vercel.app whether your anonymous device ID or Pro code has Pro. Nothing else is sent: no audio, no website names, no browsing activity.
  • Redeeming a code. When you enter a Pro code, the code is sent to the same server to be checked.
  • Feedback. Only if you use the Send feedback form (see below).

Payments

NeuraBolt Pro payments are handled by Stripe. If you choose to upgrade, your anonymous device ID is passed to Stripe as a reference so we can switch on Pro for you. We never see or store your card details. Stripe processes payments under its own privacy policy.

Third parties

We do not use advertising networks, analytics platforms or third-party trackers, and we do not sell or share your data.

Feedback

If you choose to use the in-extension Send feedback form, the message you type is sent to our server so we can read it and improve the product. An email address is sent only if you choose to add one, so we can reply. Nothing is sent until you click Send, and we never collect feedback automatically.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email info@neurabolt.ai. The full NeuraBolt privacy policy is at neurabolt.ai/privacy.

NeuraTabs Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

The short version: NeuraTabs organises your tabs on your own device. Your tab titles, full URLs and browsing history are never sent anywhere. A small amount of data does leave your device, all of it listed below: a Pro status check, a daily download of the shared category list, and, only if you deliberately correct a site's category while contributing is on, a hashed vote for that site. You can turn contributing off at any time.

What stays on your device

Almost everything is processed locally and stored in your browser's extension storage:

  • Tab grouping: tabs are categorised by a built-in heuristic that reads the tab's domain, path and title on-device. The pages you visit are never sent anywhere.
  • Group summaries: generated locally from the domains in a group. No AI service is used.
  • Learned categories: your taught and corrected categories are stored on your device and always take priority for you.
  • Snoozed tabs and saved sessions: stored in local storage and reopened by your browser. Private (incognito) tabs cannot be snoozed and are never included in saved sessions.

What leaves your device, and when

  • Pro status check: when you open the NeuraTabs popup (and when you use a Pro shortcut), the extension asks the NeuraBolt server (neurabolt-proxy.vercel.app) whether Pro is active. The request contains either a random, anonymous device ID generated by the extension or, if you have entered one, your NB-XXXX-XXXX Pro code. Never your tabs or browsing data.
  • Shared category list: about once a day NeuraTabs downloads the shared category map from the same server. This is a list of domain hashes and categories. It is downloaded even if you have turned contributing off, so you still benefit from it, and it sends nothing about you beyond the request itself.
  • Category votes (optional): see the next section.
  • Feedback (optional): see Feedback below.

The shared category brain (crowd learning)

To make grouping smarter for everyone, NeuraTabs maintains a shared map of which category a site belongs to:

  • What is sent: only when contributing is on and you deliberately correct a site's category, NeuraTabs sends three things: a SHA-256 hash of the domain (not the domain as text), a voter token, and the category you chose from a fixed list (Dev, News, Finance and so on).
  • The voter token: this lets us count one vote per device per site. It is a hash of the domain combined with a random secret that NeuraTabs creates on your device and never sends anywhere. It is not derived from the device ID used for the Pro check, so votes cannot be tied to that ID, and the tokens for different sites cannot be linked to each other.
  • Pseudonymous, not magic: a hash cannot be reversed directly, but the hash of a popular domain could in principle be matched by anyone who hashes a list of known domains. We therefore describe votes as pseudonymous rather than impossible to link to a site. We do not attempt such matching to profile anyone.
  • What is never sent: the sites you simply visit, full URLs, page content, tab titles or your history.
  • How it's used: a site only becomes a shared suggestion once at least 3 different devices have voted and 60% agree. Suggestions only help classify sites the built-in tables don't already know, and they never override your own choices.
  • Your control: you can turn off contributing in the extension's Options at any time. Removing a learned site, clearing learned categories or tapping Undo withdraws your vote for that site, even if contributing is off.

NeuraBolt Pro and payments

Payments are processed by Stripe under their own privacy policy. Only if you choose to upgrade, the extension's anonymous device ID is passed to Stripe as a reference so your purchase can unlock Pro on this device. We never see your card details.

Feedback

If you choose to use the Send feedback form, the message you type is sent to our server so we can read it and improve the product, along with the extension version. An email address is sent only if you choose to add one, so we can reply. Nothing is sent until you click Send, and we never collect feedback automatically.

What we never do

  • We never send the sites you visit, your full URLs, page content or browsing history.
  • We do not use any AI or LLM service for grouping or summaries.
  • We do not run analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • We do not inject scripts into the pages you visit.

Permissions

  • tabs and tabGroups: to read and group the tabs in your window.
  • storage: to save your settings, learned categories, snoozed tabs and sessions locally.
  • alarms: to reopen snoozed tabs at the right time.
  • contextMenus: to add the right-click "Tidy tabs" and "Snooze this tab" actions.

Contact

Questions or requests? Email info@neurabolt.ai. The full NeuraBolt privacy policy is at neurabolt.ai/privacy.

NeuraSave Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Nothing runs on your pages until you open NeuraSave

The short version

NeuraSave finds images and videos on the page you choose and saves them to your computer. Nothing from NeuraSave runs on the pages you visit until you open it and it scans the current tab. The scan runs entirely in your browser: we never receive a list of the pages you visit or what's on them. Files are saved straight to your Downloads folder. NeuraSave only contacts our server to check your Pro status, when you use the optional Pro AI Upscale feature, when you choose to send feedback, and when you choose to upgrade.

What happens locally

  • Page scanning: when you open NeuraSave (or click Rescan), it reads the images and videos already on the current tab, including any embedded frames, to build the download list. This happens only on the page you're viewing, only when you open the extension, and the results stay in your browser.
  • Downloads and conversion: the image or video you choose is fetched by your browser and saved to your Downloads folder. Optional PNG or JPG conversion happens locally inside the extension.
  • No remote code: all code runs from inside the extension. Nothing executable is loaded from the internet.

What we send over the network

NeuraSave only contacts the NeuraBolt server (neurabolt-proxy.vercel.app), and only in these cases:

  • Pro status check: when you open the popup, NeuraSave checks whether you have Pro by sending either an anonymous, randomly generated device ID (such as np_…) or the Pro code you entered (NB-XXXX-XXXX). Neither contains personal information; they identify a licence, not a person. Entering a Pro code sends that code to our server to verify it.
  • AI Upscale (Pro): when you click "2× Upscale" on an image, the URL of that image is sent to our server together with your Pro code or device ID (to check your plan and your monthly allowance of 30 upscales). Our server forwards the image URL to our processing provider, Replicate, which returns a 2x version that is saved to your Downloads folder. Only the one image you chose is sent, never your browsing history or the rest of the page.
  • Feedback (optional): if you use the Send feedback form, the message you type is sent to our server, along with an email address only if you choose to add one so we can reply. Nothing is sent until you click Send.
  • Upgrading (optional): if you choose to upgrade, your anonymous device ID is passed to our payment provider, Stripe, as a reference so your purchase can unlock Pro on this device. Payment details are handled by Stripe, never by NeuraSave.

We do not collect your browsing history, the contents of pages you visit, your name, or any other personal information. We do not sell or share your data.

Storage

NeuraSave uses Chrome's local storage to remember your anonymous device ID, your Pro code (if entered), your cached Pro status, and a daily download counter used for the free tier (10 images and 1 video per day, reset at local midnight). This data stays on your device.

Permissions

  • activeTab and scripting: to scan the page you're viewing when you open NeuraSave.
  • Host access (all sites): used only to scan the page you choose (including frames embedded in it) and to fetch the image or video you choose for saving or format conversion. NeuraSave does not run in the background on your pages or monitor sites you don't scan.
  • downloads: to save images and videos to your Downloads folder.
  • contextMenus: to add the "Save image with NeuraSave" right-click option.
  • storage: for the device ID, Pro status and daily counter described above.

Contact

Questions? Email info@neurabolt.ai. This policy is also published at neurabolt.ai/privacy.

NeuraShot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

100% local. Nothing you capture is uploaded.

The short version

NeuraShot captures screenshots and screen recordings entirely on your own device. Every screenshot, full-page stitch, region crop, PDF, and recording is created in your browser and saved straight to your computer's Downloads folder. We never see, upload, store, or transmit any image, video, or page content you capture.

What is captured

  • Screenshots are taken with Chrome's captureVisibleTab API and stitched or cropped in the browser. They exist only in memory until they are saved locally.
  • Screen recordings use Chrome's standard screen-sharing picker (getDisplayMedia) and MediaRecorder. You choose what to share every time, and Chrome shows its own sharing indicator while recording. Audio is only included if you turn it on and tick Share audio in Chrome's picker.
  • PDF export is built locally with a bundled copy of jsPDF. No external service is contacted to make a PDF.
  • No remote or CDN scripts are loaded. All code runs from inside the extension.

What we send over the network

NeuraShot only talks to the NeuraBolt licensing server (neurabolt-proxy.vercel.app), and only in these cases:

  • Pro status check: when you open the NeuraShot menu (and before a Pro feature runs), NeuraShot checks whether you have NeuraBolt Pro. The request contains either an anonymous, randomly generated device ID (such as np_ followed by random characters) or, if you redeemed one, your Pro code (NB-XXXX-XXXX).
  • Redeeming a code: when you enter a Pro code, that code is sent so it can be verified.
  • Feedback (optional): only if you use the Send feedback form and click Send. See Feedback below.

None of these requests ever contain anything you capture, the pages you visit, or your browsing history.

Payments

If you choose to upgrade, you are taken to a Stripe checkout page. Your anonymous device ID is passed to Stripe as a reference so your purchase can be linked to this device and Pro can be unlocked. Payment details are handled by Stripe and never pass through NeuraShot.

Storage

NeuraShot uses Chrome's local storage to remember your anonymous device ID, your Pro code (if redeemed), a cached copy of your Pro status (so the extension keeps working if our server is briefly unreachable), your saved format preference, and your audio-recording toggle. While a recording is in progress, its status is kept in temporary session storage that Chrome clears when the browser closes. This data stays on your device.

Permissions

  • activeTab and host access (all sites): to capture the page you're viewing (and, for All Tabs, the other tabs in the window), and to run the region-select overlay and full-page scroll logic on the page.
  • scripting: to add NeuraShot's page helper to tabs that were already open before NeuraShot was installed, so Full Page and Region work there without a reload.
  • downloads: to save your screenshots, PDFs, and recordings.
  • offscreen: to run screen recording and PDF generation in a private, local document.
  • storage: for the preferences and Pro status described above.

Feedback

If you choose to use the in-extension Send feedback form, the message you type is sent to our server so we can read it and improve the product. An email address is sent only if you choose to add one (so we can reply). This is entirely optional and is only sent when you click Send. We do not collect any feedback automatically.

Contact

Questions? Email info@neurabolt.ai. The latest version of this policy is at neurabolt.ai/privacy.
